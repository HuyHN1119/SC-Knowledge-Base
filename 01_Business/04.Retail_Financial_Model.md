# Retail Financial Model

## Purpose

Mọi hoạt động trong Retail cuối cùng đều quy về:

* Revenue
* Margin
* Cash
* Return on Capital

Supply Chain tồn tại để:

* hỗ trợ tăng doanh thu
* giảm chi phí
* tối ưu dòng tiền

---

# 1. Retail Financial Architecture

## Core Financial Flow

```text
Cash
 ↓
Inventory
 ↓
Sales
 ↓
Gross Margin
 ↓
Operating Profit
 ↓
Cash
```

Retail là vòng quay vốn.

Doanh nghiệp thắng là doanh nghiệp quay vòng nhanh hơn.

---

# 2. Retail P&L Structure

## Income Statement

```text
Revenue

- COGS

= Gross Margin

- Operating Expense

- Tax

= Net Profit
```

---

# 3. Revenue Model

## Revenue Drivers

```text
Revenue

=
Traffic
×
Conversion
×
Average Transaction Value
```

---

### Traffic

Lượng khách.

Ví dụ:

* Store Visitors
* Website Visits

---

### Conversion

```text
Conversion %

=
Transactions
/
Visitors
```

---

### Average Transaction Value

```text
ATV

=
Sales
/
Transactions
```

---

# 4. Gross Margin Model

## Gross Margin

```text
Gross Margin

=
Revenue
-
COGS
```

---

## Gross Margin %

Gross Margin %= (Revenue-COGS) / Revenue x 100%

---

## Margin Levers

### Selling Price

Tăng giá bán.

---

### Product Cost

Giảm giá vốn.

---

### Markdown Control

Giảm giảm giá.

---

### Mix Optimization

Bán nhiều sản phẩm có margin cao hơn.

---

# 5. Markdown Economics

Fashion Retail bị ảnh hưởng mạnh bởi markdown.

---

## Example

```text
Original Price = 1,000

Cost = 500

Gross Margin = 50%
```

---

Markdown 30%

```text
Selling Price = 700

Margin = 200

Margin % = 28.6%
```

---

## Key Insight

Markdown thường phá huỷ lợi nhuận nhanh hơn tăng doanh thu tạo ra lợi nhuận.

---

# 6. Inventory Economics

Inventory là tài sản lớn nhất trong Retail.

---

## Inventory Turnover

Inventory\ Turnover=\frac{COGS}{Average\ Inventory}

---

## Weeks of Supply

WOS=\frac{Inventory}{Average\ Weekly\ Sales}

---

## Inventory Productivity

```text
Higher Turn
=
Less Cash Locked
=
Higher Return
```

---

# 7. Working Capital

## Formula

```text
Working Capital

=
Inventory
+
Accounts Receivable
-
Accounts Payable
```

---

## Retail Reality

Thông thường:

```text
AR ≈ 0
```

Do khách thanh toán ngay.

Nên:

```text
Working Capital

≈

Inventory
-
Payables
```

---

# 8. Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC)

## Formula

```text
CCC

=
DIO
+
DSO
-
DPO
```

---

### DIO

Days Inventory Outstanding

---

### DSO

Days Sales Outstanding

---

### DPO

Days Payables Outstanding

---

## Best Practice

```text
Low CCC
=
Better Cash Flow
```

---

# 9. GMROI

Một trong những KPI quan trọng nhất trong Merchandise Planning.

---

## Formula

GMROI = Gross Margin / Average Inventory

---

## Meaning

```text
1 đồng tồn kho

→

tạo ra bao nhiêu đồng gross margin
```

---

## Interpretation

| GMROI | Meaning    |
| ----- | ---------- |
| <1    | Poor       |
| 1-2   | Acceptable |
| 2-3   | Good       |
| >3    | Excellent  |

# 11. Return On Inventory

## Simple Formula

```text
Return

=
Margin %
×
Inventory Turn
```

---

## Example

Case A

```text
Margin = 60%
Turn = 1
Return = 60%
```

---

Case B

```text
Margin = 40%
Turn = 4
Return = 160%
```

---

## Key Insight

Inventory Turn thường quan trọng hơn Margin đơn lẻ.
